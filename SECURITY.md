# Security reporting

Do not post customer documents, licence strings, document identities, passwords,
tokens or suspected private keys in public issues. Contact Ares Defence Labs
through the developer portal for a private reporting channel before sending
sensitive reproduction material.

Report the affected SDK/platform/version, minimal synthetic reproduction, impact
and whether it involves document parsing, licence enforcement, host callbacks,
crypto verification or distribution. Keep security-sensitive details private
until an appropriate disclosure process is agreed.

Offline document-certificate revocation and SDK licensing are different systems.
Compiled delivery and signatures protect specific integrity/entitlement boundaries;
they are not a guarantee against all reverse engineering or a hostile application
owner. See the workflow guides for exact policy and acceptance limits.
